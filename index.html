FOUNDER NOMAIR • DIRECTOR

"FROM THE LANGUAGE OF THE FRAME
TO CINEMATIC DIRECTION."

It all started with an instinctive desire to decode visual language—the realization that whoever controls the screen isn't just cutting footage, but speaking directly to the viewer, evoking genuine emotion and intent in parallel with the narrative itself.

That obsession naturally led me to become a full-time video editor, having crafted over 500+ projects across my career spanning cinematic documentaries, dramatic fiction, and narrative commercials.

The transition from editor to director was an organic, cumulative evolution: as I began writing and structuring scripts, directing became the natural progression of shaping stories from their written inception to the final frame.

Now, with all that foundation behind me, I'm here launching this project of mine: iGROW WORKS. And I don't work alone. Alongside our lead writer Marie Bu and a collaborative team of talented assistant editors, we provide creators and brands with a complete, cinema-grade creative home.

STORYTELLER MARIE BU • LEAD WRITER

"BEFORE THE FIRST CUT,
THERE IS A STORY WAITING TO BE FELT."

To me, great writing is never about complicated words or rigid formulas—it's simply about honesty. Long before the editing timeline opens, I sit down with your ideas to listen deeply and uncover what truly matters to you and your audience.

Whether it's an intimate documentary, a dramatic story, or a meaningful film for your channel, my heart is in helping you connect. I take care of the deep research, the emotional rhythm, and the gentle hooks that make viewers pause, stay, and feel something genuine.

Working side-by-side with Nomair means your authentic voice is cherished from the very first thought on a blank page to the final cut on the screen.
