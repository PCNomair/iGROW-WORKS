FREQUENTLY ASKED
QUESTIONS & PROTOCOLS

Direct, transparent answers regarding our client portal, raw footage ingestion, editorial timelines, and creative direction with Nomair & Marie Bu.

01

HOW DO WE DELIVER RAW MATERIALS AND RECEIVE FINISHED PROJECTS?

WE HAVE OUR DEDICATED PORTAL TO RECEIVE RAW MATERIALS AND RECEIVE FINISHED PROJECTS.

Every registered account receives access to our secure client portal. Once logged in, you can upload multi-gigabyte raw camera footage, audio stems, and creative briefs directly to our high-speed cloud pipeline.

When Nomair and our editorial team conclude editing, color grading, and sound mixing, your finished 4K masters, high-converting exports, and archive project files are received directly back into your private portal dashboard.

02

WHO DIRECTS AND EDITS MY VIDEO?

Nomair personally leads directorial oversight and primary video editing, backed by more than 500+ mastered projects spanning documentaries, fiction drama, and commercial campaigns.

Our lead writer Marie Bu writes narrative frameworks and tension curves. You communicate directly with the creatives shaping your film—zero agency account managers or bureaucracy.

03

WHAT IS THE EXPECTED TURNAROUND TIME?

Standard turnarounds range from 3 to 7 business days, depending on footage length, multi-cam complexity, and audio design demands.

Expedited and priority dispatch options can be requested directly inside the portal for time-sensitive releases.

04

HOW DO FRAME-BY-FRAME REVISIONS WORK?

Every draft is delivered with timecode-accurate feedback integration. You can click any timestamp, leave a note, or sketch over the frame.

Nomair reviews notes directly and addresses pacing, color grading, or pacing tweaks until the cut hits absolute cinematic perfection.

05

WHAT EDITING AND COLOR SUITES DO YOU OPERATE?

DaVinci Resolve Studio for world-class color science and mastering, Adobe Premiere Pro & After Effects for dynamic editorial rhythm and motion graphics, and professional cinematic sound design pipelines.

100% CLIENT INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

All raw camera footage, audio stems, screenplays, concept outlines, and creative briefs uploaded to iGROW WORKS or provided to Nomair remain 100% the exclusive intellectual property of the client. iGROW WORKS asserts zero copyright claim over original raw assets.

CLAUSE 02 // NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (NDA)

UNRELEASED FOOTAGE CONFIDENTIALITY

We enforce strict confidentiality on all unreleased documentary materials, narrative scripts, and rough assemblies. Client footage will never be leaked, distributed, or shared with third parties. No client project is ever published in studio showreels without prior explicit written authorization from the creator.

CLAUSE 03 // MASTER DELIVERABLES & RIGHTS TRANSFER

WORLDWIDE COMMERCIAL BROADCAST LICENSE

Upon milestone sign-off and final deliverable package transfer, all rendered 4K video exports, mastered color grades, and mixed audio tracks are transferred to the client with full, unrestricted worldwide commercial broadcast and distribution rights.

DIRECTORIAL CUT & FEEDBACK PROTOCOL

Editorial assemblies are crafted directly under the creative direction of Nomair. Revisions are executed through our structured milestone pipeline (Ingest → Rough Cut → Color & Sound → Master Delivery) to ensure precise artistic alignment.
